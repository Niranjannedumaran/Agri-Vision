Frequently Asked Questions

Practical answers regarding AgriVision's edge AI technology, flight dynamics, and pan-India agricultural deployment.

No, zero internet or cellular connectivity is required. All deep-learning computer vision models execute directly on the drone's onboard Hailo-8 edge AI computing suite. It isolates diseased foliage, weeds, and nutrient deficits, calculates 3D spray nozzle trajectories, and triggers micro-valves in under 38 milliseconds with 100% offline autonomy.
The drone pairs a high-resolution 60 FPS RGB camera with a synchronized multispectral near-infrared (NIR) sensor. Healthy crops reflect a distinctive chlorophyll signature in the 700–850 nm spectrum. When fungus, bacterial blight, or pest stress attacks, cellular transpiration shifts immediately—allowing our edge neural network to identify pathogens and weed species with 96.4% precision days before visible leaf yellowing occurs.
High-speed agronomic detection demands sub-centimeter stability at speeds up to 25 km/h. By perfecting flight telemetry and camera synchronization on our agile 4.6 kg Scout Quadcopter (TRL 4), we calibrate detection weights without the operational cost and inertia of maneuvering heavy 52 kg chemical payloads. The identical edge silicon and software stack seamlessly port onto our 25L commercial workhorse.
Permanent root-zone probes utilize sub-GHz long-range LoRa radio telemetry (868/915 MHz). Even across 5+ kilometers of rough hilly plantation terrain, probes broadcast real-time soil volumetric water content (VWC) and electrical conductivity to the base gateway. When aerial cameras detect canopy thermal stress, the system cross-checks soil data to verify whether the cause is simple water deficit or vascular fungal root rot.
Equipped with dual 16,000 mAh semi-solid state LiPo packs, the 25L Hexacopter provides 22–26 minutes of active spraying per sortie, covering 18–25 acres per charge depending on field topography. Our custom field charging trailer features quick-slide carbon latches that allow ground crews to execute battery and payload hot-swaps in under 90 seconds.
Unlike conventional hydraulic boom misters that disperse untamed clouds, our centrifugal rotary atomizers operate at low canopy altitudes of 2.0 to 3.5 meters. Downwash aerodynamic airflow drives calibrated 180–250 micron droplets directly into the crop foliage, suppressing drift in crosswinds up to 18 km/h. RTK GPS centimeter geofencing automatically disarms nozzles at field boundaries.
